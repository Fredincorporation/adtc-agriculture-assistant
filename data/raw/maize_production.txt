Maize Production Tips for Smallholder Farmers in Africa

Land Preparation:
- Clear land early.
- Plough or dig to good tilth.
- Apply organic manure (farmyard manure or compost) at 5–10 tons per hectare if available.

Planting:
- Plant at the onset of reliable rains.
- Recommended spacing: 75 cm between rows × 25–30 cm between plants (one seed per hole) or 75 × 50 cm with two seeds.
- Planting depth: 3–5 cm.
- Use improved varieties adapted to your area when possible.

Fertility Management:
- Apply basal fertilizer (NPK) at planting if affordable.
- Top-dress with nitrogen (urea or CAN) when plants are knee-high.
- Use compost, manure, and intercropping with legumes to improve soil.

Weed Control:
- First weeding 2–3 weeks after emergence.
- Second weeding before top-dressing.
- Keep fields clean to reduce pest pressure.

Common Problems:
- Fall armyworm, stem borers, streak virus, drought, poor soils.
