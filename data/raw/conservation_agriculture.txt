Conservation Agriculture and Climate-Smart Practices

Three main principles:
1. Minimum soil disturbance (reduce excessive ploughing)
2. Permanent soil cover (mulch or cover crops)
3. Crop rotation and diversification

Practical benefits:
- Better water infiltration and retention
- Reduced erosion
- Improved soil organic matter over time
- More resilience to dry spells

Entry points for smallholders:
- Keep crop residues on the field instead of burning.
- Start with mulching on smaller plots.
- Intercrop cereals with legumes.
- Use planting basins (zai pits) in dry areas.
- Gradually reduce the number of full ploughings if the soil allows.

These practices work best when combined with good weed control and reasonable fertility management.
