Weather and Climate Advisory for Smallholder Farmers

Using weather information:
- Listen to local radio forecasts or extension messages when available.
- Focus on the next 3–10 days for practical decisions.
- Long-term seasonal forecasts help with crop choice, but short-term forecasts guide daily actions.

Key farming decisions linked to weather:

1. Planting
- Plant when there is a reliable forecast of enough rain to germinate seeds.
- Avoid planting just before a predicted long dry spell.

2. Fertilizer and manure application
- Apply nitrogen fertilizer when rain is expected soon, so nutrients are not lost.
- Avoid applying fertilizer just before very heavy rain that can cause runoff.

3. Pest and disease management
- Many fungal diseases increase after prolonged wet and humid conditions.
- Scout more carefully after heavy rains or extended cloudy weather.

4. Harvesting
- Try to harvest and dry grain during clear, dry weather.
- Wet weather at harvest increases the risk of mould and storage problems.

Drought response:
- Prioritise water for the most important crops or animals.
- Use mulching and soil cover to reduce moisture loss.
- Consider early harvesting of some crops if the season is clearly failing.

Flood / heavy rain response:
- Improve drainage where possible.
- Avoid walking on very wet soils to reduce compaction.
- Watch for increased disease pressure after flooding.

Always combine weather information with local observation of your own fields and soil.
