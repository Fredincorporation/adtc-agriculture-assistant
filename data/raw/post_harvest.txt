Post-Harvest Storage for Smallholder Farmers

Main problems after harvest:
- Insects (weevils, larger grain borer)
- Rodents
- Moisture leading to mould
- Theft

Good storage practices:

1. Dry properly before storage
- Maize and cereals should be dried to about 12–13% moisture.
- Test by biting or using simple moisture methods. Grains should be hard.

2. Clean the store
- Remove old grain, dust, and cobwebs.
- Repair holes and cracks.
- Use pallets or raised platforms so bags do not touch the floor.

3. Use improved storage methods
- Hermetic bags (PICS bags or similar) are very effective against insects when used correctly.
- Metal silos (where available) also work well.
- If using ordinary bags, consider solarisation or local protectants carefully.

4. Rodent control
- Keep the store clean and sealed.
- Use traps and avoid leaving food around.
- Cats can help in some situations.

5. Regular inspection
- Check stored grain every 2 weeks.
- Remove and treat any infested bags early.

Proper drying and clean storage can save a large part of the harvest.
