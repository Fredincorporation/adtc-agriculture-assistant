Simple Farm Decision Making for Smallholders

Key questions before planting:
- How reliable is the rainfall in the coming season?
- What seed and inputs can I realistically afford?
- What is the labour availability in my household?
- Is there a market for this crop?

Risk reduction:
- Do not put all land under one crop.
- Mix food crops and some cash crops if possible.
- Keep some land under more drought-tolerant crops (sorghum, millet, cassava).

Input use:
- Only buy fertilizer or chemicals if the expected return is clearly higher than the cost.
- Micro-dosing fertilizer is usually better than broadcasting when money is limited.
- Labour is often the biggest constraint — choose enterprises that match available labour.

Record keeping:
- Even simple notes on what was planted, costs, and yields help improve decisions next season.
