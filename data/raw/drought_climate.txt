Drought and Climate-Smart Practices for African Farmers

Water Conservation:
- Mulch the soil with dry grass, leaves, or crop residues.
- Use zai pits or planting basins in dry areas (dig small pits and add manure).
- Ridge and furrow systems can help capture rainwater.
- Plant early with the first reliable rains.

Drought-tolerant options:
- Choose early-maturing maize varieties when possible.
- Grow sorghum and millet in drier areas — they tolerate drought better than maize.
- Cassava is relatively drought tolerant once established.

Other practices:
- Maintain soil organic matter (compost and manure) so soil holds more water.
- Avoid over-clearing vegetation that protects the soil.
- Intercrop to reduce risk — if one crop fails, another may still produce.

Livestock during drought:
- Reduce herd size early if possible.
- Store crop residues and hay.
- Provide shade and limited clean water.
