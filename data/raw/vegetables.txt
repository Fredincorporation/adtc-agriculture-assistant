Simple Vegetable Production for Home and Market

Important vegetables:
- Tomatoes, onions, cabbage, kale/sukuma wiki, peppers, amaranth, okra.

Key principles:
- Vegetables need more careful water and nutrient management than field crops.
- Use compost or well-rotted manure.
- Prefer raised beds in heavy soils.

Common problems:
- Pests: aphids, whiteflies, caterpillars, diamondback moth
- Diseases: blight, powdery mildew, bacterial wilt
- Water stress

Low-cost practices:
- Use compost regularly.
- Mulch to keep moisture and reduce weeds.
- Hand pick large pests when possible.
- Rotate vegetable plots.
- Use neem or soap sprays carefully for soft-bodied insects.
- Avoid overhead watering late in the day to reduce disease.
