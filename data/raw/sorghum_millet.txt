Sorghum and Millet Production for Dryland Areas

Why grow them:
- More drought tolerant than maize.
- Important for food security in low-rainfall regions.

Planting:
- Plant with the onset of rains.
- Sorghum spacing: usually 60–75 cm between rows and 15–25 cm within rows.
- Millet is often planted denser.
- Use good quality seed. Avoid damaged or old seed.

Soil and fertility:
- Both crops tolerate poorer soils better than maize.
- Respond well to manure or small amounts of fertilizer.
- Intercropping with legumes (cowpea, groundnut) is recommended.

Key challenges:
- Birds (especially on sorghum)
- Striga (witchweed) in some areas
- Stem borers and head bugs
- Drought during flowering

Management tips:
- Plant early.
- Keep fields clean of Striga if present.
- Bird scaring may be needed near harvest.
- Dry grain properly before storage.
