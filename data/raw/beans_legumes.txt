Beans and Legumes for Smallholder Farmers

Benefits:
- Provide protein for the household.
- Improve soil fertility by fixing nitrogen.
- Good for intercropping with maize or sorghum.

Common types:
- Common beans, cowpeas, groundnuts, soybeans, pigeon peas.

Planting tips:
- Plant at the right time for your area (usually with the rains).
- Avoid waterlogged soils.
- Use clean, healthy seed.
- Spacing depends on the type — follow local recommendations.

Major problems:
- Aphids, bean fly, pod borers
- Anthracnose and other fungal diseases
- Poor nodulation in some soils

Management:
- Rotate legumes with cereals.
- Avoid planting the same legume in the same field every season.
- Keep fields weed-free in the early stages.
- For some beans, staking or support can help.
